We are aware of the FAA nationwide ground stop that affected flights into SFO, OAK, and SJC on
November 13. Many flights have been cancelled. This is unfortunate.

Our event start time will not change from the
[original](/news/bmt-2026-logistics/#schedule-of-events). If affected, contact
your airline or travel company for alternatives. We are happy to change your
registration to [BMT 2026 Online](/events/bmt-2026-online/) on December 5.

For day-of updates, please use [Live](https://live.berkeley.mt/?lp). We have late
student check-in at the [info desk](/news/bmt-2026-logistics/#information-desk)
in Dwinelle. Let us know at <team@berkeley.mt> for any questions.
