If you're flying in for BMT tomorrow, today's nationwide FAA ground stop may
have delayed or cancelled your flight. The FAA stopped all flights this
afternoon after a communications outage at air traffic control centers. It's
been lifted now, but cancellations are expected to carry into the weekend, and
SFO also has long wind delays tonight. We're so sorry for the inconvenience!

**We expect BMT 2026 to start on time tomorrow, Saturday, November 14.**
Check-in still opens at **7:30 AM** in Dwinelle Hall, and the schedule in the
[Tournament Day Logistics](/news/bmt-2026-logistics/) is the same.

If your flight won't get you here in time, we've got options for you below!

## If your flight won't get you here in time

Email us at <team@berkeley.mt> with your organization, team name(s), and which
students can't make it. We'll switch them to
[BMT 2026 Online](/events/bmt-2026-online/) on **December 5** and refund the
difference.

## If you're still coming

- Check your flight status before you head to the airport.
- Plan your trip to campus ahead of time with the
  [BMT Trip Planner](/news/bmt-2026-take-transit/), especially if you're
  landing late tonight or tomorrow morning.
- Running late tomorrow? Go to the information desk in the Dwinelle Hall lobby
  (open from 8 AM) for late check-in.

## Updates

We'll post any changes on [https://live.berkeley.mt](https://live.berkeley.mt/?lp).
Add it to your phone's home screen and turn on notifications to get them right
away.

Again, we're sorry for the inconvenience. Safe travels, and we hope to see you
tomorrow!

~ BMT Coordinators
