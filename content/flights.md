We are aware of the FAA nationwide ground stop that affected domestic and some international flights, including
flights into SFO, OAK, and SJC, on November 13. On Friday afternoon, the FAA stopped flights across
the country after a communications outage at air traffic control
centers. The ground stop has since been lifted, but many flights have been
cancelled or delayed, and cancellations are expected to continue into the
weekend. SFO is also running long delays tonight due to high winds. This is
unfortunate, and we're sorry to everyone whose travel plans were affected.

BMT 2026 is still going ahead as scheduled on **Saturday, November 14**.

- Check-in opens at **7:30 AM** in Dwinelle Hall and closes at **8:30 AM**, with
  late check-in from 8:30 to 8:45 AM.
- The full schedule is in the
  [Tournament Day Logistics](/news/bmt-2026-logistics/#schedule-of-events) post.

If your flight was cancelled or delayed:

- Contact your airline or travel company directly for rebooking and other
  alternatives.
- If you can no longer make it to Berkeley in time, we are happy to change your
  registration to [BMT 2026 Online](/events/bmt-2026-online/) on **December 5**.
  Email us at <team@berkeley.mt> with your organization, team name(s), and which
  students are affected, and we'll take care of the switch.

On tournament day:

- For day-of updates, please use [Live](https://live.berkeley.mt/?lp). Add it to
  your phone's home screen and turn on notifications so you get any changes right
  away.
- If you arrive after check-in closes, we have late student check-in at the
  [information desk](/news/bmt-2026-logistics/#information-desk) in the Dwinelle
  Hall lobby, open from 8 AM and marked on the
  [Live map](https://live.berkeley.mt/map/?lp).

Let us know at <team@berkeley.mt> for any questions, and safe travels!
