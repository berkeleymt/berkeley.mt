We found the following items after BMT 2026:

- Water bottles;
- Pencil cases, stationary, and binders;
- Backpacks, tote bags;
- Jackets, sweatshirts, scarves, other items of clothing;
- Umbrellas;
- Other miscellaneous items.

If one of them is yours, please email us at <team@berkeley.mt> by November 22, 2026 with a description of the item and your mailing address, and we can ship it back to you. After November 22, 2026, we will turn over all lost and found items to UCPD.
