To all coaches, students, and parents,

Thank you for coming to BMT 2026! With over 1,900 students competing on more
than 400 teams, and a record 328 volunteers helping run the day, this was our
largest BMT yet. We're truly grateful for all the excitement, enthusiasm, and
competitive spirit every one of you brought to the tournament.

Score reports for BMT 2026 are now available on [ContestDojo].

<!-- more -->

## Score reports

On ContestDojo, students can download their **individual score report**, which
details their own performance on the General and Focus Tests and their team's
performance on the Power and Guts Rounds, with accompanying statistics. The
report also contains a scan of each answer sheet submitted, and certificates for
award winners.

Coaches can also download **team score reports**, which contain the test
details, scans, and certificates for _all_ team members, as well as a breakdown
of how the team's overall score was calculated.

As you look over your results, please remember that succeeding at BMT doesn't
mean getting everything right. Our tests are designed to have an average score
around 50%, and the true average was often even lower. When solutions are
released, take some time to go over the problems: congratulate yourself and your
teammates for the ones you solved, and try working through the ones you didn't.

As a reminder, please do not post or discuss the tests or your score report
online, or with students who did not participate, until **December 6, 2026**,
when tests and solutions will be posted to our website.
