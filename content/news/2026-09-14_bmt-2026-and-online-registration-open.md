We're excited to announce that registration for the 2026 Berkeley Math
Tournament is now open! The next iteration of our high school tournament will
be held on UC Berkeley campus on November 14, 2026.

The online version will be held on December 5, 2026, with an asynchronous
Power Round on a weekend before the live event.

To preserve fairness, **you may only register for one of these tournaments.**

<!-- more -->

## BMT 2026

Registration takes place in two phases, followed by late registration:

| Dates        | Registration Period | Price Per Student |
| ------------ | ------------------- | ----------------- |
| 9/14 – 9/27  | Phase 1             | $15               |
| 9/28 – 10/18 | Phase 2             | $15               |
| 10/19 – 11/1 | Late Registration   | $25               |

Phase 1 is limited to 48 seats per organization; shirts may be pre-ordered for
$8 per student during Phase 1 and Phase 2. Follow the links below for full
event details and the registration portal.
