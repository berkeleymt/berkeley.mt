We are excited to announce that next year's Berkeley Math Tournament (BMT)
will be on Saturday, November 14, 2026, in person at UC Berkeley!

<!-- more -->

BMT is our flagship tournament designed to challenge teams of high school
students. Younger students are welcome to participate. We recommend all
students to check out past BMT problems in
[our archive](https://berkeley.mt/archives/) to get familiar with the style
and difficulty of the tournament.

Registration will open soon! For more details, see the
[BMT 2026 Event Page](/events/bmt-2026/) and the
[BMT 2026 Online Event Page](/events/bmt-2026-online/). To stay informed, keep
an eye out for newsletters and follow us on social media!
