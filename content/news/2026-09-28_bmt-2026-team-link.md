title = "Introducing BMT Team Link for BMT 2026!"
+++

This year, we're excited to announce a new opportunity for teams at the [in-person Berkeley Math Tournament 2026](/events/bmt-2026/): BMT Team Link! Team Link pairs **students on teams with fewer than four people** with other teams, so they can compete as a team of four or more. **BMT Team Link is only available at our in-person tournament.** It will not be offered at BMT 2026 Online.

Team Link offers another way for individual students and small teams to find teammates. During BMT's team rounds (Power and Guts), students get to work with more contestants, and the team becomes more competitive for team awards ([Scoring Guidelines](/events/bmt-2026/#scoring)).

**To express interest in Team Link**, the coach can sign up on your organization's registration page, either while creating the team or afterward. Sign-ups close at the end of Late Registration.

**On Tournament Day**, BMT staff will combine teams that are interested in Team Link as they arrive. Details about the location and time will be released closer to the tournament date. Please plan to arrive at the beginning of the check-in time slot if you plan to participate. Team Link closes 15 minutes before check-in ends.

\* Student chaperones and coaches must stay on campus and be reachable for the whole event, even after their students join a Team Link team.

BMT still offers the Teammate Finder for anyone who wants to plan their team ahead of time. Any team with fewer than four people can join Team Link, including teams formed through the [BMT 2026 Teammate Finder](/bmt-2026-teammate-finder). 

\* BMT will do its best to form teams of five or six students, but every combined team will have at least four. Teams that arrive earlier during check-in are more likely to end up on a team of five or six. Teams of four are not penalized in scoring ([Scoring Guidelines](/events/bmt-2026/#scoring)).
