_Last updated: October 10, 2026_

This policy describes what information the Berkeley Math Tournament ("BMT",
"we", "us") collects, how we use it, and who we share it with. It covers this
website, our tournaments (BMT, BmMT, and their online versions), and related
services such as [live.berkeley.mt](https://live.berkeley.mt).

BMT is run by the Math Tournament at Berkeley, a student group acting
independently of the University of California. If you have any questions about
this policy, email us at <team@berkeley.mt>.

## Information we collect

### When you visit this website

This website is a static site hosted by the
[Open Computing Facility](https://ocf.berkeley.edu) at UC Berkeley. We do not
use analytics, advertising trackers, or cookies of our own. Like any web server,
our host may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses and pages
requested). Some pages load fonts, icons, and scripts from third-party content
delivery networks (rsms.me and jsDelivr), which may receive your IP address
when your browser requests those files.

### When you subscribe to our mailing list

If you sign up for our mailing list, we collect your email address. The list is
managed through [Mailchimp](https://mailchimp.com), and you can unsubscribe at
any time using the link in any email we send.

### When you register for a tournament

Registration happens on [ContestDojo](https://contestdojo.com), the platform we
use to run our tournaments. Through ContestDojo, we collect:

- **Coach and organization information**, such as the organization name and the
  names and email addresses of coaches and chaperones attending.
- **Student information**, such as student names, contact information, school
  name, test selection, team assignments, and any accessibility needs or
  accommodations you tell us about.
- **Other registration details**, such as the expected number of parents or
  spectators, fee waiver codes, and how you heard about us.

Payments are processed through ContestDojo. We never receive full card numbers;
we can see only the last four digits of the card used.

### Waivers

Before competing, each participant must sign a waiver through ContestDojo. For
in-person tournaments, the waiver includes:

- the participant's legal name and age, and signatures of the participant and a
  parent or guardian;
- emergency contact names, phone numbers, and addresses;
- physician contact information, medical insurance provider, and any medical
  conditions or medications we should know about;
- a media consent and release (see [Photos and videos](#photos-and-videos)); and
- an authorization for medical treatment of a minor.

Online tournaments require a liability waiver only.

### During a tournament

We collect your answers and scores on each test so that we can grade them,
produce score reports, and determine awards.

## How we use information

We use the information we collect to:

- run our tournaments, including assigning rooms, grading, and awards;
- send coaches and participants important information before and on tournament
  day;
- provide score reports through ContestDojo;
- respond in an emergency;
- understand our reach and improve future tournaments, using aggregate
  statistics such as school and region counts; and
- send newsletters to people who have subscribed.

## What we make public

- **Results.** We post results slides on this website after each tournament.
  These include the names of top-scoring students and teams and their
  organizations.
- **School names.** School names collected during registration are used for
  statistics and are not shown publicly. Only the organization name is shown.
- **Photos and videos.** See below.

## Photos and videos

We may take photos and videos at our in-person tournaments. Under the media
consent in the waiver, we may use them on our website and social media (such as
[Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/berkeley_mt/) and
[TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@berkeley_mt)) and in promotional materials.

## Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as needed to run our tournaments:

- **Service providers** that help us operate, including ContestDojo
  (registration, waivers, and scoring), Mailchimp (mailing list), Resend
  (tournament emails), and Google Workspace (internal grading and logistics).
- **BMT staff and volunteers**, who access participant information only as
  needed for their roles.
- **Medical personnel**, such as UC Berkeley Health Services, in an emergency.
- **Others when required by law**, or to protect the safety of participants.

## Children's privacy

Most of our participants are minors. We collect student information through
registrations submitted by coaches, or by students where allowed, and waivers
signed by a parent or guardian. Teams for BmMT, our middle school tournament,
must be registered by an adult coach. We do not knowingly collect personal
information directly from children under 13 without the involvement of a parent,
guardian, or coach. If you believe we have, email <team@berkeley.mt>.

## Data retention

We keep tournament records, including registrations, waivers, and scores,
indefinitely so that we can maintain historical results and records.

## Your choices

If you have questions or requests about your or your child's information, email
<team@berkeley.mt>. You can unsubscribe from our mailing list at any time.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the
"Last updated" date at the top of this page.
